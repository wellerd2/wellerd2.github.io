Data science frameworks for food system decision support

Across all research areas, data science provides a unifying framework for integrating environmental, microbiological, behavioral, and epidemiological data. We develop and adapt analytical tools that reflect real-world complexity and uncertainty, enabling the translation of diverse data streams into actionable insights.

Integrating diverse data streams

Understanding food safety risks requires evidence that spans scales, from microbial processes in the environment to population-level health outcomes. Our research integrates field-based sampling, laboratory analyses, and observational datasets, including environmental monitoring and epidemiological information, to capture these connections.

Systems modeling and multi-criteria decision support

Food safety challenges often involve competing goals related to environmental protection, economic viability, and public health. We use systems modeling approaches to quantify interactions, feedbacks, and trade-offs and to support more balanced, transparent, and decision-relevant analysis.

Stakeholder-centered and translational research

Our research is grounded in stakeholder-defined needs and emphasizes collaboration throughout the research process. By working closely with industry partners, government agencies, and communities, we co-develop studies and tools that are scientifically robust and practically relevant.
