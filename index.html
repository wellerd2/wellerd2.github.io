About the MODES Lab

The MODES Lab studies how environmental, food system, and surveillance data can be used to understand and manage enteric pathogen risk across food and health systems, with the goal of helping build safer, more sustainable, and more equitable food and health systems.

Work with the MODES Lab

The MODES Lab is building a collaborative research program focused on food safety, environmental health, and data science. We welcome prospective graduate students, collaborators, and partners interested in applied food safety, public health, and agri-food system analytics.
