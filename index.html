The MODES Lab

Modeling Across Agri-Food Systems & Environments

At the MODES Lab, our mission is to build safer, more sustainable, and more equitable food systems. We use food safety as a lens to study how agri-food systems function and change. By integrating systems thinking with data-driven analysis, field and laboratory research, and close collaboration with stakeholders, we translate complex evidence into actionable insights for industry, policymakers, and communities.
