The MODES Lab

Modeling Across Agri-Food Systems & Environments

The MODES Lab studies how environmental, ecological, and structural conditions shape food safety across agri-food systems. Our work integrates foodborne disease epidemiology, environmental microbiology, quantitative modeling, and stakeholder-engaged research to support safer, more sustainable, and more equitable food systems.

Explore research Contact

At the MODES Lab, food safety is both a core public health concern and a lens for understanding how agri-food systems function and change. We combine systems thinking, data-driven analysis, field and laboratory research, and collaboration with stakeholders to generate evidence that is scientifically rigorous and practically useful.

ResearchPathogen ecology, foodborne disease epidemiology, circular food systems, climate-linked risk, and food system equity. MethodsData science frameworks, public health and epidemiologic methods, systems modeling, and decision support. ProjectsCurrent work on co-managing urban farms for chemical and microbial food safety, sustainability, and economic resiliency.
