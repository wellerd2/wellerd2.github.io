The MODES Lab

Modeling Across Agri-Food Systems & Environments

We build safer, more sustainable, and more equitable food systems. Using food safety as a lens, the MODES Lab studies how agri-food systems function and change by combining systems thinking, data-driven analysis, field and laboratory research, and close collaboration with stakeholders.

Explore our research Meet the lab
Approach Systems-oriented, quantitative, translational
Focus Food safety, sustainability, resilience, equity
Methods Epidemiology, microbiology, modeling, data science

About

Our work spans microbial ecology, epidemiology, environmental surveillance, decision analysis, and agri-food systems science. We are interested in how environmental, management, and social conditions shape food safety risks and how evidence can be translated into practical actions for producers, policymakers, and communities.
