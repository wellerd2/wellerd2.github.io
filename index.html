About the MODES Lab

The MODES Lab studies how environmental conditions, human behavior, and structural factors shape food safety and public health across agri-food systems. Our work integrates foodborne disease epidemiology, environmental microbiology, quantitative modeling, and stakeholder-engaged research to support safer, more sustainable, and more equitable food systems.

Explore research Contact

At the MODES Lab, food safety is both a core public health concern and a lens for understanding how agri-food systems function and change. We combine systems thinking, data-driven analysis, field and laboratory research, and collaboration with stakeholders to generate evidence that is evidence-based and practicable for end-users.

Work with the MODES Lab

The MODES Lab is building a collaborative research program focused on food safety, environmental health, and data science. We welcome prospective graduate students, collaborators, and partners across interested in applied food safety, public health, and agro-food system analytics.
