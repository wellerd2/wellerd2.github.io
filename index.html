The MODES Lab

Modeling Across Agri-Food Systems & Environments

The MODES Lab studies how environmental, ecological, and structural conditions shape food safety across agri-food systems. Our work integrates foodborne disease epidemiology, environmental microbiology, quantitative modeling, and stakeholder-engaged research to support safer, more sustainable, and more equitable food systems.

Explore research Team and contact

At the MODES Lab, the central question is not only where hazards occur, but how risks emerge across connected systems and how evidence can be translated into better decisions. That means linking upstream environmental and management conditions to downstream human health outcomes while keeping sustainability, feasibility, and equity in view.

Research

Foodborne disease epidemiology, microbial ecology, climate-linked risk, food system equity, and the co-management of competing aims.

Methods

Public health data science, epidemiologic inference, systems modeling, decision analysis, and stakeholder-centered translational work.

Projects

Environmental pathogen surveillance, urban farm hazards, source attribution, and tools for turning complex evidence into practical guidance.

Publications

Selected peer-reviewed work spanning food safety, environmental surveillance, methods, and decision-relevant public health research.

Outputs

Profiles, code, datasets, reports, and other public-facing or translational outputs associated with the lab.

News

Selected announcements, awards, media coverage, and public-facing stories related to the lab’s work.
