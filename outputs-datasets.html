Datasets

  1. Food AI Nexus: Food AI Nexus is an open science initiative led by Cornell University, with dataset contributions from researchers at Virginia Tech and Michigan State University. Its mission is to curate and host high-quality, publicly available datasets and models dedicated to solving critical challenges in food safety and public health.
  2. Predicting Agricultural Water Quality (PAWQ) Project Datasets: The data from a survey of micorbial and physicochemical water quality for 68 waterways used for irrigation in Upstate New York.
  3. National Surface Water Antimicrobial Resistance Monitoring System Pilot Study – East Fork Watershed Data, Code, and RShiny Application
  4. Surface Water Quality Database: This collection includes multiple datasets on microbial and physicochemical water quality that were standardized into tidy formats as part of a study examining how sampling and laboratory method decisions influence our understanding of microbial dynamics in surface waters. Data were contributed by citizen science groups, academic researchers, and government agencies, and were supplemented with publicly available datasets that were compiled, curated, and harmonized for consistency.
