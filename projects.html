Projects

Current projects in the lab span environmental surveillance, urban agriculture, source attribution, and methods for translating complex evidence into practical guidance.

Environmental Pathogen Surveillance in Ireland

We are developing an environmental pathogen atlas and related analytical framework to better understand how pathogen occurrence varies across land use, hydrology, season, and other environmental gradients.

This work aims to support qualitative, uncertainty-aware risk interpretation for producers and other stakeholders within a One Health framework.

Environmental surveillance, land use gradients, and One Health interpretation.

Chemical and Microbial Hazards on Urban Farms

This project examines how chemical and microbial hazards co-occur across urban farm environments and how they may be jointly managed. Current work builds on a multi-city urban farm dataset and includes heavy metal testing, spatial analysis, and evaluation of management strategies such as raised beds.

Urban agriculture, heavy metals, microbial hazards, and co-management.

Food Safety, Sustainability, and Decision Trade-Offs

The lab is interested in how food safety interventions interact with broader goals related to sustainability, circularity, and equity. This work develops systems-based and decision-analytic approaches to identify synergies and trade-offs across agri-food systems.

Methods for Translating Complex Data into Actionable Guidance

Across projects, we are developing interpretable statistical and machine learning workflows that connect complex environmental and food system data to decision-relevant outputs. A major emphasis is on producing tools and evidence that remain useful under real-world uncertainty.
